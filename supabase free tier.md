# Supabase Free Tier Specifications

## Overview
The **Free Plan** is designed for side projects and small applications. It includes a generous suite of services to get started without needing a credit card.

* **Price:** $0 / month
* **Target Audience:** Side projects, prototypes, hobbyists.
* **Usage Limit:** If you exceed limits, services may be restricted or paused.

---

## 1. Core Services (Database & Auth)
* **Database:** 
    * **Size:** 500 MB database size limit.
    * **Pause Behavior:** Pauses after 1 week of inactivity.
* **Authentication:** 
    * **Monthly Active Users (MAU):** Up to 50,000 MAUs.
* **API:** Included for all services.

---

## 2. Storage & Realtime
* **Storage:** 
    * **File Storage Size:** 1 GB.
    * **Bandwidth:** 2 GB per month.
* **Realtime:** 
    * **Concurrent Connections:** 200 concurrent connections.
    * **Message Count:** 2 million messages per month.

---

## 3. Edge Functions & Additional Services
* **Edge Functions:** 500,000 invocations per month.
* **Dashboard:** Project management dashboard included.
* **Support:** Community support only.

---

## 4. Security & Compliance
* **Security:** 
    * **SSL:** Enforced HTTPS/SSL.
    * **Database Auth:** Native Row Level Security (RLS).
